Privacy policy

drive-backup-app is used only by its owner, on the owner's own Google account.

  • Data accessed: with the drive.file scope, only files the app itself creates in the owner's Google Drive. It cannot see any other files.
  • Use: to upload, list, read and prune the owner's encrypted backup files. Nothing else.
  • Storage: backups are encrypted before upload. The access token is kept only on the owner's own machine.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared with or sent to anyone else, and none is used for ads or analytics.
  • Revoking: remove access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Last updated: 10 November 2026.
